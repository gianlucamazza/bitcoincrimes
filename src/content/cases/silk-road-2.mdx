Silk Road 2.0 was the most visible **attempt to revive** the Silk Road model
after the 2013 takedown — same darknet marketplace pattern, Bitcoin (and
related) payments, and rapid law-enforcement focus.

## What the public record establishes

According to SDNY and WDWA:

- Silk Road 2.0 went online in **November 2013** after the original Silk Road
  seizure and Ross Ulbricht’s arrest (WDWA sentencing narrative).
- In **November 2014**, SDNY charged **Blake Benthall** (a/k/a “Defcon”) as
  operator of Silk Road 2.0; he was arrested in San Francisco.
- **Brian Richard Farrell** (a/k/a “DoctorClu”) assisted management of the site
  and was sentenced in **June 2016** to **eight years** in prison for conspiracy
  to distribute controlled substances (WDWA).

This file’s overall legal status is **convicted** because of Farrell’s sentence.
Benthall remains described as **charged** in the 2014 SDNY release cited here;
do not treat that charging announcement as a conviction without a later primary
source.

## Why it matters for Bitcoin history

SR2 shows that **dark-market demand and Bitcoin rails** did not end with Ulbricht.
It also marks the multi-staff enforcement model (operator + moderators) that
continued through AlphaBay and later markets.
