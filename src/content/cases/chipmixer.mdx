ChipMixer was a darknet **bitcoin mixing** service. In March 2023 the Justice
Department announced a coordinated international takedown and federal charges
against a Vietnamese national it identifies as the operator.

## What the public record establishes

According to DOJ’s **15 March 2023** release and related EDPA materials:

- ChipMixer was taken down in a multi-country operation.
- **Minh Quốc Nguyễn** of Hanoi, Vietnam, was charged with money-laundering and
  related offenses for operating ChipMixer.
- DOJ/complaint narrative states that since **2017** he knowingly facilitated
  laundering of about **$3 billion** worth of **bitcoin** through the service
  (agency figure).
- EDPA materials describe substantial ransomware-linked volume among other
  illicit flows during the service’s life.

Legal status is **charged** (not convicted in the cited 2023 charging releases).
Case status is **partial**: infrastructure takedown is public; person-level
trial or plea outcomes should be added when primary sources exist.

## Technical analysis

From DOJ/EDPA 2023 materials:

### Service model

- ChipMixer is described as a darknet **bitcoin mixing** service: customers send
  BTC in; the service mixes with other users’ funds and pays out to designated
  addresses, breaking simple common-input heuristics.
- Complaint narrative places ChipMixer as a successor absorber of illicit volume
  after earlier mixer disruptions (Fog, Helix, Bestmixer-class events).

### Scale claims (agency)

- Charge-era claim: since **2017**, operator facilitated laundering of about
  **$3 billion** worth of bitcoin (agency figure).
- EDPA materials also cite tens of millions of USD equivalent tied to
  **ransomware** strains among other illicit categories during the service life.

### Takedown

- March 2023 coordinated international infrastructure takedown plus federal
  **charges** (not a published conviction in the 2023 releases cited here).

## Why it matters for Bitcoin history

ChipMixer sits in the **post–Fog / Helix** mixer market: after earlier
enforcement, remaining mixers absorbed illicit volume (as the ChipMixer
complaint itself narrates). The case is a primary data point for bitcoin
laundering infrastructure in 2017–2023.

## Reading notes

- The **$3 billion** figure is an **agency charge-era valuation**, not a
  satoshi-exact independent audit reproduced here.
- Do not treat charging documents as a jury verdict.
