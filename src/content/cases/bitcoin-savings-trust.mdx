Bitcoin Savings and Trust (often associated with the moniker **pirateat40**) is
an early, fully Bitcoin-native **investment fraud**: not a dark market, but a
Ponzi dressed as a high-yield Bitcoin business.

## What the public record establishes

According to the U.S. Attorney’s Office for the Southern District of New York:

- In **November 2014**, SDNY announced securities and wire fraud charges against
  **Trendon Shavers**, describing them as the first federal criminal securities
  fraud charges in a **Bitcoin-related** scheme.
- In **September 2015**, Shavers **pleaded guilty** to securities fraud.
- In **July 2016**, he was **sentenced** for operating a Bitcoin Ponzi scheme
  (SDNY announcement).

## Why it matters for Bitcoin history

The case shows that Bitcoin crime is not only dark markets and exchange hacks:
**securities fraud theory** reached pure Bitcoin investment schemes early, and
SDNY treated BTC-denominated investor harm as a federal securities matter.
